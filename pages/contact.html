Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for bulk purchases?

Our minimum order quantity varies by product. For vegetables, it's typically 100kg, for fruits 50kg, and for grains 500kg. Contact us for specific product MOQs.

Do you provide export services?

Yes, we provide comprehensive export services to various countries. We handle documentation, quality certification, packaging, and logistics for international shipments.

How do you ensure product quality?

We have strict quality control measures including farm inspections, testing labs, proper storage facilities, and cold chain management. All products undergo quality checks before dispatch.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, UPI payments, cheques, and for international orders, we accept wire transfers and letters of credit. Payment terms can be negotiated for bulk orders.

How can farmers become partners with KisanBox?

Farmers can join our network by contacting us directly. We provide support for quality certification, training, and guaranteed purchase agreements. We work with both individual farmers and farmer producer organizations.
