Government Schemes for Farmers

Subsidies, insurance, and financial support you may be eligible for

Central Govt

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

Direct income support of ₹6,000/year in three installments to all farmer families.

Eligibility: All land-holding farmers
Benefit: ₹6,000/year
Check Status / Apply
Central Govt

PM Fasal Bima Yojana

Crop insurance scheme providing financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage due to unforeseen events.

Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops
Premium: 1.5-5% of sum insured
Apply Before Kharif Cutoff
Maharashtra Govt

Micro-Irrigation Scheme (MIS)

Subsidy up to 55% for small/marginal farmers on drip and sprinkler irrigation systems.

Eligibility: Small & marginal farmers
Subsidy: Up to 55% on equipment cost
Apply Online
APEDA

Organic Certification Assistance

Financial assistance for obtaining NPOP and PGS-India organic certification, training, and market linkage.

Eligibility: Farmers practicing organic methods
Benefit: Up to ₹50,000 assistance
